The Evolution of Web Design

Web design has transformed dramatically since the early days of the internet, moving from static pages to dynamic, interactive experiences.

In the beginning, websites were simple text documents with minimal styling. As CSS matured, designers gained the ability to control layout, color, and typography with precision.

Today, responsive design has become the standard, ensuring websites look great on every device, from desktops to smartphones.

Modern typography relies heavily on font pairing, spacing, and hierarchy to create readable and visually appealing content for users.
