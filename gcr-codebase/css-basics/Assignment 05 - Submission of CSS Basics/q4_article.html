My Journey into Web Development

By Kashish Varshney | Published: August 29, 2026

Learning web development has been one of the most rewarding experiences of my academic journey. It started with simple HTML tags and slowly grew into understanding complex layouts and responsive designs.

The first challenge I faced was understanding how the box model works. Once that concept clicked, everything else about spacing and layout started making more sense to me.

CSS opened up a whole new world of possibilities, from simple color changes to complex animations. I realized that design and logic go hand in hand when building a good webpage.

Working on real projects helped me apply theoretical knowledge in practical ways. Building a portfolio site taught me about responsive design and how important it is for modern websites.

Looking ahead, I am excited to explore JavaScript and backend development to become a complete full-stack developer.

"The expert in anything was once a beginner who never gave up."
