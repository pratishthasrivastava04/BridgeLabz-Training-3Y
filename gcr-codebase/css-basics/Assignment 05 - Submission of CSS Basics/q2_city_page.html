Overview

Manali is a beautiful hill station in Himachal Pradesh, known for its scenic mountains and pleasant climate throughout the year.

Top Attractions

Solang Valley, Rohtang Pass, and Hadimba Temple are among the most visited spots in Manali.

Weather

Manali experiences cold winters with snowfall and pleasant summers, making it a year-round tourist destination.
